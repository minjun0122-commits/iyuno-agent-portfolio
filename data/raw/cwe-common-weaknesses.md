CWE is a community-maintained catalog of the general categories of software
and hardware weaknesses that can lead to vulnerabilities, distinct from CVE,
which tracks specific vulnerability instances in specific products. Frequently
cited entries include CWE-79 (cross-site scripting), CWE-89 (SQL injection),
CWE-20 (improper input validation), CWE-200 (exposure of sensitive
information), and CWE-284 (improper access control). Security teams use CWE
identifiers to classify findings consistently across tools and vendors, and
the annual "CWE Top 25 Most Dangerous Software Weaknesses" list, derived from
aggregated CVE and exploitability data, is commonly used to prioritize secure
coding training and static-analysis rule sets.
